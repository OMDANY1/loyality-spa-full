# Section Animations

The website should never feel static.

Each section should have its own animation identity.

---

# Hero

Image slowly zooms.

Headline fades upward.

Buttons fade in.

Background moves slightly slower than content.

---

# Featured Services

Cards reveal one after another.

Images scale gently.

Hover reveals additional details.

---

# Why Loyalty Spa

Icons animate on entry.

Statistics count up.

Background patterns move subtly.

---

# Products

Cards stagger.

Images zoom.

Price fades.

Quick Add slides in.

---

# Membership

Cards float slightly.

Glow effect on hover.

Benefits animate sequentially.

---

# Testimonials

Horizontal scrolling.

Auto pause on hover.

Fade transitions.

---

# Gallery

Masonry fade.

Image zoom.

Fullscreen lightbox transition.

---

# Blog

Cards stagger upward.

Featured article slightly larger.

---

# Footer

Fade upward.

Social icons lift on hover.

Newsletter input expands on focus.

---

# Global Motion Rules

No element should animate more than once unnecessarily.

Animations should never block interaction.

Motion should support storytelling rather than decoration.

Respect reduced-motion user preferences.
