# Accessibility Standards

Follow WCAG 2.2 AA.

---

## Navigation

Keyboard accessible.

Visible focus.

Skip to content.

---

## Images

Meaningful alt text.

Decorative images marked correctly.

---

## Forms

Labels for every input.

Error messages.

Success feedback.

Logical tab order.

---

## Contrast

Minimum AA contrast ratio.

Readable typography.

Avoid relying on color alone.

---

## Motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Disable non-essential animations.
